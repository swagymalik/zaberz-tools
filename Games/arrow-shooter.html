About This Game

Arrow Shooter is a classic bow and arrow target game. Aim your bow at the moving targets, time your shot perfectly, and rack up the highest score. Each round gives you 10 arrows — the closer to bullseye, the more points you earn!

How to Play

  1. 1 Move your mouse (or finger on mobile) to aim the bow.
  2. 2 Click or tap to shoot an arrow toward the target.
  3. 3 Hit the center for max points. 10 arrows per round!
