About This Game

Flappy Bird is the legendary arcade game that took the world by storm. Navigate a little bird through sets of pipes without touching them. Each set of pipes you pass earns you a point. Simple to play but hard to master — how far can you go?

How to Play

  1. 1 Tap the button, press Space, or click anywhere to make the bird fly up.
  2. 2 Navigate through the gaps in the pipes without touching them.
  3. 3 Each pipe you pass = 1 point. Don't hit the ground or pipes!
