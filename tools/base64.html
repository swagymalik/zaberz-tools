About This Tool

The Zaberz Base64 Encoder & Decoder converts text or files into Base64 format and back, correctly handling Unicode characters like emojis and non-Latin scripts. It also supports URL-safe encoding for use in links and tokens, and can encode entire files — such as images — into a Base64 string ready to embed directly in HTML or CSS. All conversion happens locally in your browser.

How to Use

  1. 1 Type or paste text, then click Encode or Decode.
  2. 2 For files, upload one below to get its Base64 output.
  3. 3 Copy the result or download it as a .txt file.
