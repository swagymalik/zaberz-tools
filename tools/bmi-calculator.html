About This Tool

The Zaberz BMI Calculator estimates your Body Mass Index using the WHO-standard formula, supporting both metric and imperial units. It shows your result on a visual gauge alongside the healthy weight range for your height. Note: BMI is a general screening measure and doesn't account for muscle mass, bone density, age, or sex — it isn't a diagnosis. For personalized health advice, please consult a doctor or qualified healthcare provider.

How to Use

  1. 1 Choose Metric or Imperial units.
  2. 2 Enter your weight and height.
  3. 3 Click Calculate to see your BMI, category, and healthy weight range.

Frequently Asked Questions

According to WHO standards, a BMI between 18.5 and 24.9 is considered a normal, healthy weight range for most adults.

BMI is a general screening tool and doesn't distinguish between muscle and fat. It may be less accurate for athletes, older adults, pregnant women, or very muscular individuals. Consult a healthcare provider for a personalized assessment.

BMI is calculated as weight in kilograms divided by height in meters squared, or as 703 times weight in pounds divided by height in inches squared.
