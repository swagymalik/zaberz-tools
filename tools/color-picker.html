About This Tool

The Zaberz Color Picker lets you pick any color and instantly get its HEX, RGB, and HSL codes. It also generates a set of tints and shades for building consistent UI palettes, plus complementary and analogous color suggestions to help you build a balanced color scheme for your designs.

How to Use

  2. 2 Copy the HEX, RGB, or HSL value you need.
  3. 3 Explore shades, tints, and harmony suggestions for your palette.

Frequently Asked Questions

HEX and RGB both describe a color by its red, green, and blue components, just in different formats. HSL describes a color by hue, saturation, and lightness, which can be more intuitive for adjusting a color's brightness or vibrancy.

A shade is a color mixed with black to make it darker, while a tint is a color mixed with white to make it lighter. Both are generated automatically from your selected color.

A complementary color sits opposite your chosen color on the color wheel, creating strong visual contrast, which is often used to make design elements stand out.
