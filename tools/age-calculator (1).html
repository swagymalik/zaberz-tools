About This Tool

The Zaberz Age Calculator works out your exact age in years, months, and days, along with fun extras like your total days lived, the day of the week you were born, a countdown to your next birthday, and your zodiac sign. Everything is calculated instantly in your browser, and your date of birth is never stored or shared.

How to Use

  1. 1 Select your date of birth using the date picker.
  2. 2 Click Calculate Age.
  3. 3 See your full breakdown, birthday countdown, and zodiac sign.
