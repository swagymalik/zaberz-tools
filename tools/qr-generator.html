About This Tool

The Zaberz QR Code Generator creates scannable QR codes entirely in your browser — nothing you type is sent to a server. Customize the size, colors, and error correction level, then download your QR code as a PNG image ready to print or share.

How to Use

  1. 1 Type or paste the text, link, or info you want to encode.
  2. 2 Adjust size, colors, and error correction if needed.
  3. 3 Click Generate, then download your QR code as a PNG.
